# Customer Portal

Embed the Freemius Customer Portal on your site so customers can manage subscriptions, licenses, billing, and downloads. Use the **Freemius Customer Portal** block in the block editor.

## Setup

1. Create or edit a page, then add the **Freemius Customer Portal** block.
2. Enter your **Store ID** and **Public Key**. Find both in the [Freemius Developer Dashboard](https://dashboard.freemius.com/) under **Stores** — open your store, then **Settings → Keys**.
3. Optionally set **Preview height** under **Portal Settings**. This controls the editor preview only; on the live site the portal resizes to fit its content.
